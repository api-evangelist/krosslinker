# Krosslinker

> Krosslinker (KrossLinker) is a Singapore-based deep-tech advanced-materials manufacturer that designs and produces silica aerogels — among the most insulating solids known — using its proprietary, water-based "Aspire" chemistry and manufacturing platform. Krosslinker has no public API, developer portal, or machine-readable API artifacts; this file describes the company for agents that encounter it in the API Evangelist network.

## Company

- [Website](https://krosslinker.com): Company site, products, and applications.
- [Products](https://krosslinker.com/products/): Cryar aerogel board, QuintAero aerogel powder, KrossShield SR Pro cooling paint, aerogel blanket (in development).
- [Applications](https://krosslinker.com/applications/): Cold-chain packaging, building and construction insulation.
- [Careers](https://krosslinker.com/careers/): Open roles; office at 8 CleanTech Loop, CleanTech III, Singapore 637145.
- [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/company/krosslinker): Company updates.
- [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCnpDIQVQc601YHMGy1OL3nQ): Product and process video.
- Contact: connect@krosslinker.com
